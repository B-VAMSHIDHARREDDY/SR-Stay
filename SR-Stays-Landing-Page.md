- **Icons:** Line-style icons in Red or Black only, no gradients besides the logo itself.
- **Buttons:** Rounded corners (8–12px radius), matching the logo's rounded-square icon shape.
- **No additional colors** (no blue/green/yellow) — strictly Red, Black, White throughout the entire page for full brand consistency.
